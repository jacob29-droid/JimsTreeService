Our Services

From one dead limb to a full lot clearing, here’s what we handle.

Tree Removal

Dead, dying, leaning or storm-damaged trees, including the dangerous ones close to houses, fences and power lines. We take them down in sections when there’s no room to drop them.

Trimming, Pruning & Dead-Wooding

We remove dead and crossing limbs, raise the canopy and clear branches off the roof. Proper cuts keep the tree healthy for years.

Stump Grinding

We grind stumps below grade so you can mow, plant or build right over the spot.

Brush Chipping & Hauling

We chip the brush, load the wood and haul it all off. Cleanup is part of the job, not an extra.

Oak Wilt Prevention, Diagnosis & Treatment

Oak wilt is a real problem in our part of Texas. Joey can check your trees, tell you what’s going on and treat the ones that can be saved.

Storm & 24-Hour Emergency Service

Trees and limbs don’t wait for business hours to come down. Call any time and we’ll get out there.

Our Equipment

60-Foot Bucket Truck

Reaches tall trees and limbs hanging over homes safely, without tearing up your yard.

Grapple Truck

Lifts the big logs and heavy brush piles that are too much to handle by hand, and hauls them away.
