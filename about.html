About Jim’s Tree Service

A Stephenville family business since 1969.

Where It Started

Jim’s Tree Service was started in 1969 by Jim Prichard. He built the business one job at a time, the old-fashioned way: show up when you say you will, do the work right and treat every yard like it’s your own. That’s how he earned his reputation around Stephenville.

We lost Jim, but his name is still on the trucks, and the business still runs the way he ran it.

Who Runs It Today

Today the business is owned and run by Jim’s son, Joey Prichard. Joey carries on what his dad started, with the training to back it up. He’s an ISA Certified Arborist and a degreed horticulturist, so he knows how to take a tree down safely and how to keep a good one healthy.

When you call, you’re talking to the family, not a call center.
